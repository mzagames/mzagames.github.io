Pengdoku privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026
Developer: MZA Gaming
App: Pengdoku: Penguin Logic Puzzle
Android package: com.mzagaming.pengdoku
Contact: mzagames@proton.me
Public policy: https://mzagames.github.io/games/pengdoku/privacy.html

1. Scope

This policy describes Pengdoku version 0.4.0 for Android and its web version. Pengdoku is an offline logic puzzle game suitable for all ages. It has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat and no user-posted content.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress, unfinished puzzle, score, hints and peeks, daily-puzzle calendar, unlocked penguins, rescue album and settings (sound, music, vibration, Auto ✕, timer, coordinates) in the app's local storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to MZA Gaming or anyone else. MZA Gaming does not run any server, analytics or cloud-save service for this game.

3. No data collection or sharing

The Android app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send information over the network. It does not collect or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or any other data. It contains no advertising or analytics software. The only permission it requests is vibration, used for short haptic feedback that you can switch off in Settings.

4. Google Play updates

When the game opens, it asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version of Pengdoku is available. This check is handled by Google Play under Google's own policies; the game does not send it to MZA Gaming. If an update is available you can choose Update now or Cancel.

5. Children

Pengdoku can be played by children. Because the game collects no personal information and shows no ads, no information about children is collected. A parent or guardian may contact mzagames@proton.me with any privacy question.

6. Removing your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game. Any of these removes all saved information, because it only exists on your device.

7. Support

If you email support, your email provider and our mailbox process your email address and message so we can answer. Please do not include unnecessary personal information, especially about a child.

8. Changes

If a future version adds features that handle information differently (for example ads or online features), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
