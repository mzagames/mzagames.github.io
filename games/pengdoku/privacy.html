Pengdoku privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 (version 0.5.0)
Developer: MZA Games
App: Pengdoku: Penguin Logic Puzzle
Android package: com.mzagames.pengdoku
Contact: mzagames@proton.me
Public policy: https://mzagames.github.io/games/pengdoku/privacy.html

1. Summary

Pengdoku has no account and no sign-in, and MZA Games does not run any server that receives your game data. From version 0.5.0 the free Android game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase. We never sell your data.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves your puzzle progress, unfinished puzzle, score, stars, hints and peeks, daily-puzzle calendar, unlocked penguins and themes, rescue album, settings (sound, music, vibration, Auto ✕, timer) and an unsent feedback draft in the app's local storage on your device. It also keeps a count of levels since the last ad, used only to space ads out. This information is not sent to MZA Games. Android may include it in your own device backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Android and your Google account, not by MZA Games.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android game shows a small banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never during a puzzle, and not in the first levels), and optional reward videos you choose to watch for 3 extra hints, 3 extra peeks or an extra heart.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise (where allowed), measure and protect ads against fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect and share with Google and its partners: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers; approximate location from your IP address; and interactions with ads (for example views and taps), app and device information, and crash and performance diagnostics. This data is encrypted in transit. It goes to Google, not to MZA Games. Google's use of it is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences (PG). The web version of Pengdoku shows no ads.

4. Your choices

EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you play, Google's consent form asks whether ads may use your data. You can consent, choose not to consent, or manage options, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → "Privacy & ad choices". US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for ads in Settings → "Privacy & ad choices". Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

5. Remove ads purchase

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase called "Remove ads" through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment, so MZA Games never receives your card or payment details. The game only receives Google Play's record that the Google account on the device owns the purchase, keeps a yes/no note of it on the device, and asks Google Play again when it starts so the purchase comes back after a reinstall (or with Settings → Restore purchase) and is withdrawn if it is refunded. Once bought, Pengdoku shows no ads, and the extra hints, peeks and hearts you used to watch a video for become free. Google Play's terms and the Google Privacy Policy apply to the payment. Stars earned in the game and the items in the in-game shop cannot be bought with money.

6. In-game feedback form

Settings has a Send feedback form. When you press Send, it sends only the category you pick, your message, the game version, your Android version and phone model (or, on the web, your browser name) and, if you type one, an e-mail address for our reply. It travels over an encrypted connection to a small relay run by MZA Games on Google Apps Script, which e-mails it to mzagames@proton.me. Google hosts the relay and the e-mail under its own policies. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and an unsent draft stays only on your device. We use feedback only to reply to you and to improve Pengdoku, keep it only as long as needed for that, and never sell it. Please don't include personal details you don't want to share. To have a message deleted, e-mail mzagames@proton.me.

7. Permissions

The Android game uses the internet and network state to load ads and send feedback, the advertising ID permission for ads as described above, and vibration for short haptic feedback (you can switch it off in Settings). It does not use precise location, the camera, microphone, contacts or your files.

8. Google Play updates

When the game opens, it asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether a newer version of Pengdoku is available. Google Play handles this check under Google's policies; it is not sent to MZA Games. If an update is available you can choose to update now or later.

9. Children

Pengdoku is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, contact us and uninstall the game; you can also reset the device's advertising ID.

10. Removing your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game to remove everything saved on your device. Ad data held by Google is covered by Google's policies and your advertising ID settings. To delete a feedback message you sent, e-mail mzagames@proton.me.

11. Support

If you email support, your email provider and our mailbox process your email address and message so we can answer. Please do not include unnecessary personal information.

12. Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released, and update the Play Store data safety section to match.
